# Interest statement

Source of truth: "Political Economy Digest: Setup Drafts" doc. Keep this file in sync when the doc changes.

## Core topics (flag strongly)

- Mass preferences over trade and globalization: how voters form trade attitudes, and the role of information, cues and heuristics.
- Political trust, trust in leaders and partisan cues as information shortcuts for economic policy preferences.
- Immigration attitudes and their link to fiscal outcomes: perceived fiscal burden, welfare chauvinism, tax and spending preferences.
- Public opinion on economic policy in Latin America, including Chile.
- Work on multinationals and citizen preferences for multinationals. Specifically anything about preferences over FDI, supply chains, and mobile capital.

## Adjacent topics (flag if the design or data is relevant)

- Compensation and embedded liberalism, the backlash against globalization, the China shock and its political effects.
- Redistribution preferences, fiscal contracts and tax morale.
- Elite cues and media effects on economic attitudes.
- Latin American political economy more broadly: commodity cycles, the left turn and its aftermath, party systems and economic voting.

## Methods and data of interest

- Survey experiments (conjoint, vignette, information treatments).
- Large-scale observational analysis of cross-national surveys: LAPOP AmericasBarometer, Latinobarómetro, ISSP, WVS, and new merged opinion datasets.
- New datasets on trade, tariffs, fiscal policy or migration that cover Latin America.
- Formal models and structural estimation.

## Exclusions

None set.
